Manali

About the Destination

Manali is a beautiful hill station in Himachal Pradesh known for its snow-capped mountains, scenic valleys, and adventure activities.

Highlights

  • Snow-covered mountains
  • Adventure sports
  • Beautiful valleys
  • Pleasant weather

Top Attractions

  • Solang Valley
  • Rohtang Pass
  • Hadimba Temple
  • Mall Road

Best Time to Visit

October to June

Estimated Budget

₹15,000 - ₹25,000 per person
