Jaipur

About the Destination

Jaipur, the Pink City of India, is famous for its royal palaces, magnificent forts, colorful markets, and rich Rajasthani culture.

Highlights

  • Historic forts and palaces
  • Traditional Rajasthani culture
  • Colorful local markets
  • Delicious cuisine

Top Attractions

  • Amber Fort
  • Hawa Mahal
  • City Palace
  • Jantar Mantar

Best Time to Visit

October to March

Estimated Budget

₹11,000 - ₹25,000 per person
