Goa

About the Destination

Goa is India's most famous beach destination, known for its golden sandy beaches, vibrant nightlife, Portuguese heritage, and delicious seafood.

Highlights

  • Beautiful beaches
  • Water sports activities
  • Vibrant nightlife
  • Portuguese architecture

Top Attractions

  • Baga Beach
  • Calangute Beach
  • Dudhsagar Falls
  • Basilica of Bom Jesus

Best Time to Visit

November to February
