Ooty

About the Destination

Ooty, also known as the Queen of Hill Stations, is a beautiful destination in Tamil Nadu famous for its tea gardens, pleasant climate, rolling hills, and scenic beauty.

Highlights

  • Lush tea plantations
  • Cool and pleasant weather
  • Beautiful lakes and gardens
  • Scenic mountain views

Top Attractions

  • Ooty Lake
  • Botanical Garden
  • Doddabetta Peak
  • Nilgiri Mountain Railway

Best Time to Visit

October to June

Estimated Budget

₹8,000 - ₹20,000 per person
